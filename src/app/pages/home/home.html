Recording tips

  • Same setup every time. Same mic, distance and room — consistency matters more than perfection.
  • Read like you talk. Warm and natural, the way you’d speak to a client on the phone.
  • Say exactly what’s written. If you slip, retake — or flag it in review.
  • Rest your voice. About two hours a day keeps your voice consistent.
